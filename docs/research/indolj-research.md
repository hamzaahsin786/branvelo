## What it is
Pakistan-based SaaS platform for food businesses (restaurants, cafés, dessert shops, bakeries).
Core pitch: commission-free direct online ordering — the restaurant gets its own branded
website (desktop/mobile) + iOS/Android app for takeaway, delivery, dine-in, drive-in, curbside
pickup, so customers order direct and the restaurant avoids ~30% marketplace commissions.

## Products (6 pillars + services)
1. Online Ordering System — branded site + apps; POS/KDS integration; online payments + cash;
   promotions/coupons/combos; QR-code menus; SMS remarketing; dynamic menu scheduling;
   location-wise delivery charges/zones; branch/menu management; analytics dashboards;
   customer feedback; 24/7 support.
2. POS — QR ordering, quick billing, dine-in/takeaway/delivery, table management, KDS,
   discounts/promos, sales reports, staff roles, customer management, cloud multi-location sync,
   offline/hybrid modes; "affordable hardware and printer options."
3. Delivery Fleet Management — rider management + auto-assignment, live customer tracking
   (status + ETA), route optimization, cash/digital collection by riders, performance analytics,
   Bykea integration.
4. Loyalty & Engagement — points/cashback/discounts/free items, automated SMS/email/push,
   behavior-based targeting, POS-synced rewards.
5. Digital Menu Boards — dynamic updates, daypart scheduling, images/video/promos, centralized
   multi-branch control. Software only; restaurant provides Google TV/dongle + screens + Wi-Fi.
6. Self-Ordering Kiosk — touchscreen, multi-language, upsells, feedback, cash/card/wallet/QR payments.
Also: Digital Marketing (SEO/PPC), SEO-friendly sites, Google Analytics, 3 Facebook banners,
custom banner design, free cloud hosting + SSL, free updates/support.
Order intake channels: email, web dashboard, Android tablet app, SMS; new-order alerts by
phone/email/SMS.

## Process / timelines
Lead form (name, email, phone, restaurant name, city, # locations, services wanted) → Indolj team
builds the branded site + sets up the menu free → live in 2–5 business days (depends on menu
complexity + client providing materials) → restaurant manages via web dashboard. No limits
claimed on menu items/customers/orders. Cancel anytime. Multi-location/franchise supported.
Own domain or subdomain.

## Model notes
- Pure SaaS/subscription (monthly + 1% rev share), not white-label resale: each restaurant gets
  its own branded site built by Indolj's team on Indolj's platform/cloud, managed via dashboard.
- Go-to-market = productized onboarding (free setup, fast launch) + subscription + tiny revenue
  share, sold on the "escape foodpanda commission" pitch.

## Implications for a Muhammad-built equivalent
- Hard parts are operational, not technical: restaurant onboarding (menu setup, photos, training,
  in Urdu, by phone), 24/7 support expectations, sales motion.
- COD dominates PK food ordering → payments gateway can wait.
- Sensible MVP: multi-tenant platform → templated Kababjees-style branded site per restaurant
  + shared admin dashboard (menu + order management) + order alerts via WhatsApp/SMS/email +
  COD + JazzCash/Easypaisa (manual). Skip fleet, kiosks, hardware POS at start.
- Pilot candidate: Usama Burgers (data + design reference already gathered).
